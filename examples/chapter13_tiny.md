## Chapter 13 First order differentiation

## A Ultratangent space

13.1. Theorem. Let $X$ be a complete length CAT space. Then $T_pX$ is CAT(0).

Proof. This follows by taking blowups and passing to the ultralimit.

13.2. Corollary. The tangent space has nonpositive curvature.
